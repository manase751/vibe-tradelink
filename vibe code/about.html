Good questions

Frequently asked

What is Trade Link?

Trade Link is a directory and conversation platform for suppliers, wholesalers and small businesses to discover each other directly.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan includes a business profile, browsing and search, plus a limited number of messages.

What does Pro include?

Pro is planned to include unlimited messages, a featured listing, a verified badge, product analytics and priority support.

How do I get verified?

Verification is a planned review process. Details will be shared when the platform is ready to accept applications.

How do I contact you?
